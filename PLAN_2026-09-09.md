## The finding that frames arcs 1 and 3

**The platform computes far more than it shows.**

`web/public/data/` ships exactly: `sibc_l1_annotations.json` (96 cards), `atm_pos_insights.json`
(32 cards), the two `*_planes.json` sidecars, `opportunities_feed.json`, and chart data.

`system_state_{period}.json` — `entity_states`, `force_states`, `edge_states`, `loop_states`,
**`mix_states`** — is computed every ingestion, validated by both gates, and **never leaves
`analysis/`**. The only Layer 2 that reaches a browser is 4 cross-system items rendered inline
as "Deep".

So "I can't tell what value the layers are bringing" is not a perception problem. Most of
Layer 2 has never been rendered once.

Arc 1 is unshown **computation**. Arc 3 is unsourced **context**. Same problem, two sides.

### The design call: add a tier, do not redesign

This would be the third dashboard redesign since July. The read mode and the §15 cut contract
are sound and should not be reopened.

The missing thing is a **tier above the reads**: a standing state line per dimension, present
every period whether or not anything is news.

Today the top tier is "what's news" — **29 of 96** SIBC cards score as reads this period
(planes: 29 read / 17 composition / 50 subject; payments 9 / 1 / 22). Twenty-nine cards is not
an answer to "what is happening".

Target shape (illustrative — **needs an approved ASCII pass before any code**, per the
CLAUDE.md authoring rule):

```
Main sectors — credit growing 19.3% YoY, accelerating.
Mix steered toward Services (+5.8pp), away from Personal Loans.
Coherent: 4 of 4 sectors moving together.
```

One sentence, same shape at every cut, every period, computed not chosen. Reads sit below it.

### Precondition, now met (2026-09-10)

The causal layer this arc will surface was **not fit to publish** when this plan was written: 11 of
18 force excerpts did not appear on the page they cited, every one labelled `inference`. It is now
18 of 20 verifying, the other 2 correctly hypotheses. SIBC also gained 2 sourced PSL forces
(education, renewable) and the NBFC why. Arc 1 can go ahead without putting unverifiable claims on
screen.

### Why this answers the "what value do the layers bring" question

It makes the layer boundary legible instead of invisible: **L1 is the speed, L2 is whether
anyone is steering.** A reader can see the two doing different jobs in one line.

### Constraints that carry over

- **The regime WORD only, never the coherence number** (decision #4 — no gate can ground a
  number that lives in a state file rather than signals.db; invented 0.73 and 0.31 both PASSED).
- **The pairing rule holds**: a share is never rendered without that entity's speed and
  acceleration. A falling share of a faster-growing flow is the most misreadable number here.
- **ASCII layout first, explicit approval, then implement.**
- Cards NEST, never merge — Check 2g / 4c / 5.7 / 5.8 untouched. Presentation layer only.

### Likely build shape

1. Ship `mix_states` to the browser as a compact sidecar (mirror `stamp_planes.py`:
   precompute, `--check` freshness guard, wire into both manifests + `check_derived_fresh`).
2. Derive the state line deterministically from movement rows + mix state.
3. Render as the standing tier in `ReadModeShell` (shared — both pipelines inherit).
4. Gate it: the line's numbers must trace (it quotes YoY and tilt, both in signals.db);
   the regime word is a word, not a number.

## Arc 2 — the next ingestion pipeline

### The three-way question, answered

**DBIE** is not a choice. It is the *portal* the RBI series arrive through, not a source.
Already established; not a decision point.

**Bank quarterly results / presentations — reject as source #3.** Three reasons:
(a) PDF-only, and this repo has **no PDF extraction capability of any kind** — that is its own
project, not an ingestion; (b) bank decks are unstructured and inconsistent between issuers, so
they validate nothing about whether the manifest generalises; (c) per-bank credit exposure is
**STRBI-annual-only** — monthly per-bank credit never comes from RBI. Highest effort, lowest
architectural return.

**Cluster B, price of credit — recommended source #3** (repo, WALR, WADTDR, G-sec, WACR).
Unchanged from `PLAN_2026-08-11.md`, and the reasoning has strengthened:

- It forces **exactly one** new concept: **cadence**. The registry has no frequency field —
  0 of 266 signals — and everything assumes monthly (`csv_yoy_streak` counts periods-as-months,
  prose says "16-period range"). Price series are weekly and quarterly. That is the real blocker
  for 2 pipelines → 22 sources, and this surfaces it in isolation rather than tangled with
  geography axes and PDF parsing.
- Highest narrative return per unit of work. The platform measures **quantity** only.
  Quantity + price answers *"is credit growth slowing because demand fell, or because it got
  expensive?"* — the causal question the differentiator claims to answer and currently cannot.
- No new entity axis, no PDF, structured at source.

### Order

1. **Cadence field first** — cheap, no new data, unblocks everything after it.
2. **Cluster B price of credit** = source #3. Design on 2 pipelines, *validate* with this one.
3. **BSR-1 quarterly** = source #4 — introduces the geography / rate-slab entity axis.
4. PDF extraction is its own project, scheduled only when a source worth it demands it
   (11 of 21 remaining P-list sources are behind it).

### Collision to remember

Arc 3 wants **per-entity** (bank/NBFC) information. Arc 2's macro sources cannot supply it —
bank-level credit is annual-only. These are different axes and must not be conflated.

### The build: an RBI regulatory watch, joined to the model

The framing that keeps this from being a feed: **a watch over one authoritative publisher,
joined to the system model.**

Every piece of machinery already exists and is gated:
- `core/source_fetch.py` — the one URL→text path (HTML → visible text; PDF via `pdftotext -layout`)
- the **49-host** allowlist (sidbi.in T2 and nabard.org T1 added 2026-09-10). ⚠️ Its census
  (19 ok / 11 blocked / 11 unreachable / 6 inconclusive, 2026-08-19) is **load-bearing for this
  arc and should be re-run before the build** — a probe bug produced a false "blocked" reading
  on 2026-09-09, so a census predating that correction cannot be assumed sound. NPCI *is*
  genuinely blocked (re-confirmed properly); PIB and RBI are open.
- `bank_sourcing.excerpt_on_page` — the trust anchor
- the temporal-validity check (it already caught RBI/2024-25/119 partially rolling back an
  earlier circular)

What changes is only the **direction**: S4 today *pulls* (asks a question, hunts a source).
This *pushes* (watches RBI's index, asks what each item attaches to).

### What the 2026-09-09/10 sourcing pass proved about this design

Four findings from actually doing the work by hand, all of which shape the build:

1. **Index by "what changed in the window", not "find evidence for hypothesis Y".** S4 named an
   education interest-subvention and an MNRE rooftop-solar subsidy; searching for either would have
   failed, and MNRE is not allowlisted. The real instrument for both — plus a third force — was one
   document, the RBI PSL Directions 2025. **The watch should enumerate instruments by effective date
   and then ask what they attach to.** That is the opposite of S4's pull, and it is why push works.
2. **Search, not fetching, is the bottleneck — and search engines are unreliable here.** `site:`
   queries failed on both DuckDuckGo and Bing; headline-shaped queries worked. RBI and PIB publish
   their own dated indexes, which is what the watch should read rather than a search engine.
3. **The watch MUST carry the excerpt audit from day one.** The force store accumulated citations
   nobody could check for months — 11 of 18 failed when finally tested — precisely because the trust
   anchor was never pointed at the existing store. A feed that writes claims must verify them on a
   schedule, not once at authoring. `guards/audit_force_sources.py` is the pattern.
4. **An instrument is not a statistic.** NABARD's SHG-linkage highlights are a published series, so
   by the spec's own external-AND-non-observable test they are a candidate SIGNAL, not a force. The
   watch needs that classification step or it will file statistics as drivers.

### The admission rule — this is the whole design

**An item enters only if it attaches to an entity or channel in the system model.**

- A co-lending circular attaches to `services → NBFCs`. It enters.
- An ATM interchange circular attaches to the payments model. It enters.
- Anything about a market this platform does not ingest is **logged as unattachable and dropped**.

That constraint does real work: the regulatory layer can only ever tell you things about credit
you already measure. It is what stops the sprawl into news.

Same gate as every other sourced claim: URL + verbatim excerpt verified on the page + effective
date + attachment point. Nothing auto-promoted.

### Why this is sequenced before arc 2

It closes arc 1's loop. Layer 2 can currently say *"main sectors steered toward Services
+5.8pp"* and **cannot say who is steering**. A sourced force is what makes L2 answer *why*.
Regulatory watch is that input.

### Explicitly not building

A news reader · an LLM summarising press coverage · anything ingesting bank commentary.
The moment an item can enter without a model attachment point, it is an aggregator.
