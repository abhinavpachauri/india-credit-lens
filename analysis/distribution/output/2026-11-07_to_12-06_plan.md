### 1 · Sat 7 Nov · Bank credit
> Bank credit reached ₹229.9L Cr in Sep 2026, up 19.4% over the year and up ₹6.1L Cr in the month.
> Both the level and the growth rate are the highest in our series. Where did it go? Tomorrow's posts follow it.

### 2 · Sun 8 Nov · Medium-sized firms
> Bank credit to medium-sized firms grew 33.2% in Sep 2026, its fastest in our series. Large firms: 16.0%.
> Medium firms hold 10.3% of industry credit and took 15.8% of the new.

### 3 · Mon 9 Nov · Services
> Services is 28.3% of bank credit but took 38.9% of the new credit in Sep 2026. It grew 28.3%, its fastest
> yet, against 19.1% for non-food credit.

### 4 · Tue 10 Nov · Construction
> Bank credit to construction grew 30.0% in Sep 2026, its fastest yet, and sped up 5.31 points in a month.
> Total industry credit: 19.5%. Order books, or working capital stretching?

### 5 · Wed 11 Nov · HDFC vs SBI at shops
> Credit card value at shops, Sep 2026. Market +7.2%. SBI +25.2%. HDFC Bank −6.7%, from +36.1% a year ago.
> HDFC still leads at 26.5%, SBI is at 18.3%. Base effect, or a real change?

⚠ The base must be named: HDFC peaked in this same month last year.

### 6 · Thu 12 Nov · CPI day: card balances
> Credit card balances grew 7.2% in Sep 2026. Net of CPI (+5.5% over the year) that is 1.6%. Cards are
> 4.1% of personal loans and took 1.9% of the new.

⚠ Sep CPI is quoted, even if Oct CPI lands that morning.

### 7 · Fri 13 Nov · Gold loans
> Gold loans grew 82.6% in Sep 2026, or 73.0% net of CPI (+5.5%). They are 7.8% of personal loans and took
> 23.9% of the new. Collateral price, or demand?

### 8 · Sat 14 Nov · Fertiliser
> Bank credit to fertiliser makers grew 53.1% in Sep 2026, its fastest yet. Net of WPI fertiliser prices
> (+11.6%) that is 37.2%. Working capital for a subsidy cycle, or new capacity?

### 9 · Sun 15 Nov · Swipes vs rupees
> Credit cards, Sep 2026. At shops: swipes +23.4%, value +7.2%. Online: transactions +22.3%, value −12.5%.
> More transactions, smaller bills.

⚠ Online value fell hard against a large Sep 2025 (SBI was +64.1%, ICICI +43.0% then), and HDFC's share

### 10 · Mon 16 Nov · Petroleum
> Bank credit to petroleum, coal and nuclear fuels grew 9.3% in Sep 2026. Net of WPI mineral oils (+40.0%
> over the year) it fell 21.9%. More rupees, less fuel financed.

### 11 · Tue 17 Nov · UPI QR
> UPI QR codes, Sep 2026: Yes Bank 72.1%, down from 77.4% a month earlier. Axis Bank 21.9%, up from 16.0%.
