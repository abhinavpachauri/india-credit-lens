September's RBI card data: the government banks are taking the rupees.

1. We keep swiping. The rupees are not keeping up.

Credit card swipes at shops are up 23.4% in a year. The rupee value is up only 7.2%. In September alone, value growth slowed from 9.1% to 7.2%, while swipes barely moved (23.5% to 23.4%).

2. Public sector banks took most of the growth.

They have 22.6% of card value at shops, but took 71.0% of its growth. A month ago that was 51.0%. Private banks hold 72.7% and grew just 1.4%.

3. SBI up, HDFC down.

SBI grew 25.2%. HDFC Bank, still the biggest at 26.5%, fell 6.7%, after growing 0.9% in August. Part of that is a high base: HDFC was growing 36.1% a year ago.

4. Small finance banks are adding cards fast.

Only 2.1% of all cards, but 12.3% of the new ones, up 117.4% to 26.94 lakh.

And the question I can't answer yet: private bank swipes are up 21.6% with value up 1.4%. Public bank swipes are up 13.6% with value up 26.6%. Why are the two moving so differently?
