September's RBI data: the new money goes to lenders.

1. Services took most of the growth.

Services is 28.3% of bank credit but took 38.9% of its growth over the year. Its yearly growth rose from 24.3% in August to 28.3%. Non-food credit grew 19.1%.

2. Inside services: NBFCs.

Bank credit to NBFCs grew 41.8%, the fastest in our data (37.5% in August), to ₹22.53L Cr. NBFCs are 34.7% of bank credit to services and took 46.4% of its growth.

3. Not all of it is the NBFCs you'd think of.

Public financial institutions grew 79.7% and took 24.7% of the growth in bank credit to NBFCs. Housing finance companies grew 19.7%.

4. What NBFCs lend: retail.

NBFC credit grew 15.4% (15.8% in August). Retail is 43.5% of it and took 58.6% of the growth, up from 57.8%.

5. Inside retail: gold.

NBFC gold loans grew 66.1% and took 30.9% of retail's growth, more than housing (20.1%).

The question I can't answer yet: is bank money now funding NBFC retail, and gold? RBI's NBFC data covers the larger NBFCs and HFCs, and bank credit to NBFCs includes the PFIs, so the two don't line up one to one. If you work at an NBFC, what am I missing?
