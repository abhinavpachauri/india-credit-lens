September's RBI credit data against MoSPI's output and prices: credit is running ahead.

1. Chemicals: credit up 25.5%, output down 1.4%. Credit growth rose from 23.5% in August, and output slipped further from −0.9%.

2. All engineering: credit up 36.0%, output up 12.5%. Credit growth rose from 33.4% in a month.

3. Power: credit up 22.9%, output up 6.0%. Electricity prices (WPI) fell 0.4%, so real growth is higher still at 23.4%.

4. Petroleum goes the other way. Credit is up 9.3% in rupees, down from 14.8% in August. Oil prices (WPI mineral oils) are up 40.0%, so in real terms credit fell 21.9%.

5. Gold loans are up 82.6%, or 73.0% after consumer prices (CPI, +5.5%). In a month it barely moved, from 83.2%.

Is this capacity being built ahead of output, or more debt per rupee of output? Both can be true, and we haven't tested which.
