September's card scorecard for India's 9 biggest private banks.

Who's adding cards? Federal Bank, up 75.0% in a year, and IDFC First, up 23.7%. HDFC Bank, the biggest, is at 8.3%.

Who's getting the spending at shops? HDFC still leads with 26.5% of the market, but its value fell 6.7% (it grew 0.9% in August). A year ago it was growing 36.1%, so the base is high. Kotak, Yes Bank and Federal Bank all grew over 35%.

More swipes, fewer rupees at HDFC: swipes up 17.7%, value down 6.7%. What's going on there?

Online is messier. Private banks' online value fell 10.4%, from +3.4% in August. ICICI fell 31.4% and Axis 29.0%, against big numbers last year. HDFC went the other way at +15.7%, and its share has jumped two months running.

IndusInd is down on every measure here.

Credit cards only, from RBI's monthly data. No loans or NPAs in this file, and I'm not saying why any of it happened. If you work on cards at one of these banks, what am I missing?
