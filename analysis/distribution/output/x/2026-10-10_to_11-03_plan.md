### 3 · Mon 12 Oct · HDFC card spend at shops
> HDFC Bank's credit card spend at shops grew 0.9% in Aug 2026. The market grew 9.1%. A year ago
> HDFC was growing 28.3%, peaking at 36.1% in Sep 2025. Its share is 27.4%, down from 30.8%.

⚠ The base effect is named in the post. Share fell 30.8% → 26.6% in one month (Nov → Dec 2025);

### 5 · Wed 14 Oct · Gold loans: banks and NBFCs both
