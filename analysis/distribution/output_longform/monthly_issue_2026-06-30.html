Where the mix is shifting

Within services lending, the mix is tilting toward Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs). Its slice of services credit grew by 3.4pp over the year, while Other Services gave up 2.1pp. Put simply — a bigger share of every rupee lent to services now goes to Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

Fleet vs usage

A few things that usually move together have come apart this year:

The number of debit cards and spending on them have pulled apart this year. The number of debit cards moved by +2.8% while spending on them moved by -4.3% — a gap of 7.1pp, which is the space between having a card and using it.

The POS machines deployed and the money flowing through them have pulled apart this year. The POS machines deployed moved by -0.5% while the money flowing through them moved by +4.2% — a gap of -4.7pp, which is the space between how many machines there are and how much they handle.

The ATMs deployed and the cash withdrawn from them have pulled apart this year. The ATMs deployed moved by -2.7% while the cash withdrawn from them moved by -6.1% — a gap of 3.3pp, which is the space between the size of the ATM fleet and how much it dispenses.
